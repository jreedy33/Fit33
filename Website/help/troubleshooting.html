Troubleshooting

📖 8 min read Last updated: Jul 2026

Something not working? Most issues fall into one of the buckets below. Work through the matching section — and if you're still stuck, the contact info is at the bottom.

1. Data Isn't Syncing

Your workouts, meals, and stats sync to the cloud whenever you're signed in. If something looks missing:

  1. Check that you're signed in — open your Profile; if you see a sign-in prompt, sign back in and your data will re-sync.
  2. Check your connection — most data queues locally when you're offline and retries automatically once you're back online, but a few social features need a live connection.
  3. Give it a minute — friend activity, challenge progress, and leaderboards typically update within seconds, but can lag briefly on a poor connection.

My Strava run isn't showing in Fit33

Strava activities usually arrive within about a minute of finishing. If they stop appearing:

  1. Open Settings → Strava and confirm it still shows as connected
  2. If it's disconnected (tokens can expire), reconnect and re-authorize on Strava's site
  3. Confirm the activity actually posted to Strava first

Will workouts double-count from Strava and Apple Health?

No — Fit33 dedupes across sources. The same workout arriving via both Strava and HealthKit is saved and credited only once.

2. Notifications Aren't Arriving

Check both layers of settings:

  1. iOS level: Settings → Notifications → Fit33 → make sure "Allow Notifications" is on
  2. App level: Fit33 → Settings → Notifications → check the per-category toggles (workout reminders, hydration, quests, streaks, challenges, social)

Also worth checking:

  • Focus modes (Do Not Disturb, Sleep, Work) can silence notifications — add Fit33 to your allowed apps
  • Scheduled Summary (iOS Settings → Notifications) can delay notifications into a digest
  • Low Power Mode can delay background delivery

3. Steps / Weight / Workouts Show Blank or Zero

This is almost always a HealthKit permission issue — for example if you tapped "Don't Allow" at first launch, or revoked access later:

  1. Open iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Fit33
  2. Turn on the data categories you want Fit33 to read (steps, workouts, weight, heart rate, etc.)
  3. Return to Fit33 — data backfills on the next sync

If you'd rather check from inside the app: on newer iOS versions Health permissions also appear under iOS Settings → Apps → Health → Data Access & Devices → Fit33.

4. "My Streak Disappeared"

The two usual causes:

  • Timezone changes: streak days follow your device's local calendar. Crossing timezones near midnight can make a day appear "missed" — if you believe your streak was lost incorrectly, report it via Settings → Report a Bug so we can investigate.
  • A genuinely missed day: that's what Streak Shields are for — they protect your streak on days you can't train. Check the streak widget to see your shields.

Remember that cardio counts too: a walk logged in Fit33, Apple Health, or Strava keeps your cardio streak alive.

5. Subscription & Purchase Issues

I paid but Premium isn't active

  1. Make sure you're signed in to the App Store with the same Apple ID you purchased with
  2. Open the upgrade screen in Fit33 and use Restore Purchases
  3. If it still doesn't unlock, contact us with your purchase receipt (Apple emails it to you)

How do I cancel my subscription?

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone
  2. Tap your name → Subscriptions
  3. Find Fit33 and tap Cancel Subscription

Your access continues until the end of the billing period. Refunds are handled by Apple — request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

6. The App Crashed

Sorry about that. Crash reports reach us automatically, but a bug report helps us fix it much faster:

  1. Update to the latest version of Fit33 and iOS first — many crashes are already fixed
  2. Go to Settings → Report a Bug
  3. Include what screen you were on and what you tapped right before the crash — screenshots help a lot
💡 Mid-run crash? If the app quits during an outdoor run, just reopen it — Fit33 snapshots your in-progress cardio session and restores it automatically (within a 4-hour window).

7. "I Can't Find My Food"

Food search is powered by the USDA database, which doesn't carry every branded product. Try searching the generic equivalent ("greek yogurt" instead of a specific brand), or point the camera at the product's nutrition facts panel — the scanner reads the label text directly.

8. Still Stuck?

Reach out and we'll get you sorted:

  • In-app: Settings → Report a Bug
  • Email: info@doublethr33s.com

Include your device model, iOS version, the screen you were on, and what you did right before the problem — it cuts the back-and-forth in half.
