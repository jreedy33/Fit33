Workouts & Exercises

📖 8 min read Last updated: Jul 2026

The Workout tab is the heart of Fit33. This guide covers the three ways to get a workout, how the active workout screen works, how to swap exercises without losing progress, and where your history lives.

1. Three Ways to Get a Workout

Surprise Me

One tap, one complete workout. Fit33 instantly generates a balanced session based on your fitness goal, experience level, available equipment, any limitations you've told us about, and your recent workout history (so you don't repeat the same session twice in a row).

Generate Workout

Want more control? The generator lets you set the parameters — target muscle groups, duration, and intensity — and then builds the session for you. Everything the generator picks respects your equipment and any injury or limitation filters from your profile.

Build Custom

Hand-pick every exercise yourself:

  1. Go to the Workout tab
  2. Tap "Build Custom"
  3. Search and add exercises from the library
  4. Set your sets, reps, and order (drag to reorder)
  5. Tap GO! to start

Custom workouts can be saved and reused, and shared with friends when you're done.

💡 "Why did I get this exercise?" Auto-generation weighs your goal, equipment, experience, and history. If a pick isn't for you, swap it (see below) or use Build Custom for full control. Marking exercises as Favorites also nudges future generations toward the movements you love.

2. The Exercise Library

Fit33 ships with a library of 6,000+ exercises, each with a video demonstration, step-by-step instructions, and muscle targets. You can:

  • Filter by muscle group (chest, back, legs, etc.)
  • Filter by equipment (barbell, dumbbell, machine, bodyweight, and more)
  • Save Favorites for quick access
  • View your history with each exercise, including your best sets

3. The Active Workout Screen

Once you tap GO!, you're in the active workout. For each exercise you'll see the video demonstration, suggested weight and reps (based on your history), and your previous performance. To log a set:

  1. Enter the weight you used
  2. Enter the reps you completed
  3. Tap ✓ — the rest timer starts automatically

During the workout you can also:

  • Add extra sets with the + button
  • Mark a set as a warmup (doesn't count toward volume)
  • Add notes for future reference
  • Build supersets to pair exercises back-to-back
  • Drag to reorder the remaining exercises

4. Swapping an Exercise

Machine taken? Movement doesn't feel right? You can swap any exercise — before or during the workout:

  1. Tap the exercise's menu and choose Swap
  2. Fit33 suggests alternatives that target the same muscle group with equipment you have
  3. Pick a replacement and keep going
✅ Your logged sets are safe: swapping an exercise mid-workout preserves any sets you've already completed. The swap only replaces the remaining work.

5. Warm-Ups & Stretching

  • Smart Warm-Up: Fit33 can generate a warm-up automatically based on the muscles your workout targets — do it before you touch your working weight.
  • Stretch Mode: a guided stretching routine for after your session (or any time).

6. Finishing a Workout

Tap Finish Workout when you're done. Your summary shows total duration, total volume lifted, personal records achieved 🏆, and a calories-burned estimate. The workout is saved automatically and synced to the cloud.

After finishing, Workout Replay gives you coaching insights on the session — volume analysis, plateau detection, and context around any PRs you hit.

7. Workout History

Every completed workout lives in your history in the Workout tab. Tap any entry for the full detail view — exercises, sets, weights, and how it compared to previous sessions. History also powers the weight suggestions you see during future workouts.

8. Rest & Recovery Days

On rest days, Fit33 offers a personalized Recovery Day — stretching, mobility, and foam-rolling work matched to what you've trained recently. Recovery days are a great way to stay active without interfering with your training.

9. Common Questions

The workouts feel repetitive — what can I do?

The generator tracks what you've done recently and prioritizes fresh exercises, but your equipment selection can limit the pool. Adding more equipment in your profile, swapping exercises, and favoriting new movements all increase variety.

Can I work out at home?

Yes — set your workout location to Home in your profile and select the equipment you have (or just bodyweight). All generation will use only what's available to you.

How are weight suggestions calculated?

From your own history with that exercise. The more workouts you log, the smarter the suggestions become.
