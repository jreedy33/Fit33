Getting Started with Fit33

📖 8 min read Last updated: Dec 2024

Welcome to Fit33! This guide will walk you through everything you need to know to start your fitness journey with our smart workout app.

1. Creating Your Profile

When you first open Fit33, you'll go through a quick onboarding process. This helps Fit33 understand your fitness level and goals:

  • Fitness Goal: Choose your primary objective (build muscle, lose fat, get stronger, etc.)
  • Experience Level: Be honest here – it helps us recommend appropriate exercises
  • Workout Location: Gym or Home – this determines available equipment
  • Available Equipment: Select what you have access to
  • Days Per Week: How often you plan to work out
💡 Pro Tip: You can always update these settings later in your Profile. As your fitness level improves, update your experience level to unlock more challenging exercises!

2. Understanding the Dashboard

Your Dashboard is your fitness command center. Here's what you'll find:

Quick Actions

  • Surprise Me: Get an instant personalized workout
  • Generate Workout: Customize your workout parameters
  • Build Custom: Hand-pick exercises yourself

Your Stats

The dashboard shows your weekly activity, streak, and recent workouts at a glance.

3. Starting Your First Workout

Ready to sweat? Here's the fastest way to start:

  1. Tap "Surprise Me" on the Dashboard
  2. Review the generated workout (you can swap exercises if needed)
  3. Tap the big GO! button
  4. Follow along and log your sets

During your workout, you'll see:

  • Exercise name and video demonstration
  • Suggested weight and reps (based on your history)
  • Rest timer between sets
  • Your previous performance for that exercise
📊 Smart Suggestions: The more workouts you complete, the smarter our recommendations become. Fit33 learns your strength levels and suggests appropriate weights.

4. Logging Your Sets

Accurate logging is key to tracking progress. For each set:

  1. Enter the weight you used
  2. Enter the reps you completed
  3. Tap ✓ to log the set
  4. Rest timer starts automatically

You can also:

  • Add extra sets with the + button
  • Mark a set as a "warmup" (doesn't count toward volume)
  • Add notes for future reference

5. Finishing Your Workout

When you're done, tap Finish Workout. You'll see:

  • Total duration
  • Total volume lifted
  • Personal records achieved 🏆
  • Calories burned estimate

Your workout is automatically saved and synced to the cloud.

6. Next Steps

Now that you've completed your first workout, explore these features:

  • Smart Programs: Follow a structured training plan
  • Exercise Library: Browse 7,000+ exercises
  • Progress Stats: Track your gains over time
  • Favorites: Save exercises you love
🎯 Stay Consistent: The key to results is consistency. Try to complete at least 3 workouts per week for the first month!
