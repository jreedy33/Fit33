Smart Programs

📖 6 min read Last updated: Jul 2026

Single workouts are great — but consistent progress comes from structured training. Smart Programs are multi-day plans built specifically for you, with progression baked in so every week builds on the last.

1. What Is a Smart Program?

A Smart Program is a personalized training plan that runs for 7, 14, 21, or 30 days. Instead of following a cookie-cutter template, every training day is dynamically generated from your profile:

  • Your fitness goal (strength, hypertrophy, fat loss, endurance, and more)
  • Your experience level
  • Your available equipment and workout location
  • Your schedule — how many days per week you can train
  • Any limitations or injuries you've told us about

2. Starting a Program

  1. Open the Workout tab
  2. Choose Smart Programs
  3. Pick your program length (7 / 14 / 21 / 30 days) and goal
  4. Fit33 generates the full plan — training days, rest days, and all

Your active program shows up as a widget on your Dashboard, so today's workout is always one tap away.

3. How Progression Works

Smart Programs are built around three training principles:

  • Progressive overload: intensity increases over the course of the program so you're always challenged appropriately.
  • Periodization: volume and intensity are structured across the plan rather than being random from day to day.
  • Deload periods: longer programs include lighter stretches that let your body recover and adapt — they're a feature, not a bug.
💡 Feeling an easy day? That's likely a planned deload or lighter session. Trust the structure — recovery is where the adaptation happens.

4. Rest Days Inside a Program

Programs schedule rest days based on the days-per-week you chose. On those days, the app can offer a personalized Recovery Day — stretching, mobility, and foam-rolling matched to what you've been training — so rest days never feel empty.

5. Programs and the Rest of the App

  • Program workouts are logged exactly like any other workout — sets, reps, weights, rest timer, exercise swaps.
  • Completed program days count toward your streak, daily quests, and weekly league points.
  • Your active and completed programs sync to the cloud with the rest of your data.

6. Common Questions

Can I still do other workouts while on a program?

Yes. Surprise Me, the generator, custom workouts, and cardio all remain available. The program tracks its own days independently.

Can I swap exercises inside a program day?

Yes — the same swap flow works inside program workouts. Suggestions match the same muscle group with your equipment, and completed sets are preserved.

Which program length should I pick?

If you're new to structured training, start with a 7-day or 14-day plan to build the habit. The 21-day and 30-day programs give periodization more room to work and suit users who already train consistently.

What happens when the program ends?

The completed program moves into your history, and you can start a fresh one immediately. Because each plan is generated from your current profile, your next program reflects any progress or profile changes you've made.
