Frequently Asked Questions

Creating a custom workout is easy! Here's how:

  1. Go to the Workout tab
  2. Tap "Build Custom" or the + button
  3. Search and add exercises from our 7,000+ library
  4. Set your reps, sets, and rest times
  5. Tap "GO!" to start your workout

You can also save your custom workouts for future use!

The Surprise Me feature instantly generates a personalized workout based on:

  • Your fitness goals (build muscle, lose fat, etc.)
  • Your experience level
  • Your available equipment
  • Your workout history (to ensure variety)

It's perfect when you want a quick, effective workout without planning. Just tap and go!

Smart Programs are AI-powered training plans that adapt to you:

  1. Choose a program based on your goal (strength, hypertrophy, etc.)
  2. The AI generates your daily workouts based on your profile
  3. As you complete workouts, the program adapts to your progress
  4. Progressive overload is automatically built in

Programs typically run 4-12 weeks and include periodization for optimal results.

Absolutely! Fit33 works great for home workouts:

  • Set your workout location to "Home" in your profile
  • Select the equipment you have (or just bodyweight)
  • All workout generation will use only your available equipment
  • We have 1,000+ bodyweight exercises alone!

You can switch between home and gym modes anytime.

Fit33 automatically tracks your personal records (PRs):

  • Every completed set is recorded
  • When you beat a previous best, you'll see a 🏆 PR! badge
  • View all PRs in the Stats section
  • See 1RM estimates for strength exercises

We track both weight PRs and volume PRs (total weight × reps).

Yes! Your data is automatically synced to the cloud:

  • All workouts are backed up in real-time
  • Sign in on any device to access your data
  • Your workout history, stats, and programs sync automatically
  • Even if you lose your phone, your data is safe

Just make sure you're signed in with your account.

You can manage your subscription through Apple:

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone
  2. Tap your name at the top
  3. Tap Subscriptions
  4. Find Fit33 and tap it
  5. Tap Cancel Subscription

Your access continues until the end of your billing period. You can resubscribe anytime.

We love hearing from you! Here's how:

  • In-app: Go to Settings → Report a Bug
  • Email: info@doublethr33s.com
  • Feature requests: Same email! We read every message.

When reporting bugs, screenshots and steps to reproduce are super helpful!
