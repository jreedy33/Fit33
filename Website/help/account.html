Account & Settings

📖 7 min read Last updated: Jul 2026

Everything about managing your Fit33 account: how sign-in works, what lives in Settings, the privacy controls you have, and how to export or delete your data.

1. Signing In

Fit33 supports four sign-in methods:

  • Email & password
  • Sign in with Apple
  • Continue with Google
  • Continue with Facebook

Your account is the key to your data — sign in on any device and your workouts, stats, and programs come with you. Forgot your password? Use the "Forgot Password?" link on the sign-in screen.

💡 Tip: If you signed up with Apple or Google, always use that same button to sign back in — it's linked to the same email behind the scenes.

2. Phone Verification

During onboarding you can verify a phone number. It's used for account recovery and trust — not for marketing calls:

  • A one-time code is sent via SMS (45 countries supported)
  • You get up to 3 attempts per verification
  • Only the verification state is stored — never the SMS itself

3. Your Profile

From your Profile you can edit:

  • Display name, username, and profile photo
  • Fitness goal, experience level, and days per week
  • Workout location and available equipment
  • Physical limitations and injuries (these filter workout generation)

Updating your goal or equipment immediately changes what the workout generator and Smart Programs build for you.

4. Settings Overview

  • Units — switch between metric (kg/km) and imperial (lb/mi)
  • Notifications — per-category toggles (workout reminders, hydration, quests, streaks, challenges, social)
  • Health integrations — Apple Health, Strava, Fitbit, WHOOP, Oura, InBody
  • Cloud Backup — your data syncs automatically while signed in
  • Download Data — export everything tied to your account
  • Report a Bug — send us a description, screenshot, and device info
  • Privacy & Security — visibility controls and blocked users (below)

5. Privacy Settings

Settings → Privacy & Security controls what other users can see about you. Changes sync to our servers and are enforced there — they apply everywhere, instantly:

  • Hide Profile Photo
  • Hide Friend Activity — your workouts stop appearing in friends' feeds
  • Hide from Weekly League — opt out of league leaderboards
  • Hide from Contact Sync — friends can't discover you via their contacts
  • Hide from Search — your profile won't appear in user search
  • Hide Active Status

6. Blocking & Reporting Users

You can block or report anyone, from anywhere their content appears:

  • Long-press a message in a challenge chat or a card in the friend-activity feed → Report & Block
  • Open someone's profile → Block

Blocking removes the user immediately from your feeds, chats, and suggested friends — they can't message you, see your activity, or compete with you. Reports go to our moderation team for review.

To see or undo your blocks: Settings → Privacy & Security → Blocked Users → Unblock.

7. Exporting Your Data

Go to Settings → Download Data to export your personal data — workouts, meals, stats, and profile information.

8. Deleting Your Account

You can permanently delete your account and all associated data:

  1. Open Profile → Delete Account
  2. Confirm the deletion
⚠️ This is irreversible. All personal data is permanently removed from our servers within 30 days. If you think you might come back, consider exporting your data first.

9. Common Questions

Is my data backed up?

Yes — your data syncs to the cloud in real time while you're signed in. A lost or new phone just means signing back in.

What happens to my subscription if I delete my account?

Deleting your Fit33 account does not cancel an active App Store subscription — manage that separately in iOS Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

How do I change my email?

Contact us at info@doublethr33s.com and we'll help you update the email on your account.
