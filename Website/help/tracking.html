Progress, Tracking & Integrations

📖 9 min read Last updated: Jul 2026

Fit33 tracks a lot more than sets and reps: nutrition, hydration, weight, steps, and — if you connect them — data from your favorite wearables. This guide covers where everything lives and how the integrations work.

1. The Stats Tab

The Stats tab is your progress headquarters:

  • XP & Levels — earn XP from workouts, daily quests, and challenges
  • Workout charts — volume, frequency, and trends over time
  • Muscle heatmap — a visual of which muscle groups you've been training (and neglecting)
  • Personal records — auto-detected PRs for weight and volume, with 1RM estimates for strength exercises
  • Achievements — milestone unlocks as you progress
  • Body composition — weight and body-comp history

2. Nutrition Tracking

The Meals tab handles food logging and macro tracking:

  • Food search — powered by the USDA FoodData Central database
  • Label scanner — point the camera at a product's nutrition facts panel and Fit33 reads the numbers straight off the label (on-device, the photo is never uploaded)
  • Macro tracking — daily calories, protein, carbs, and fat against your targets
  • Recipes — browse recipes or import one from a URL
  • Saved meals & shopping list — save frequent meals for one-tap logging and build a shopping list from your plan
  • "What to eat right now" — suggestions aware of the time of day and the macros you still need
📷 About the scanner: the camera reads the nutrition-facts panel text, not the barcode. If a product's label won't scan, search the product name instead. If USDA doesn't carry a niche branded product, logging the closest generic equivalent works well.

3. Hydration, Weight & Steps

  • Hydration: the water widget on your Dashboard logs daily intake with a tap.
  • Weight: log weigh-ins manually or let them sync automatically from Apple Health. Your trend shows on the Dashboard and in Stats.
  • Steps: synced from Apple Health, with a daily step goal and activity-ring visualization.

4. Connecting Apple Health

Apple HealthKit is the backbone integration — it syncs steps, workouts, active energy, heart rate, weight, and body composition both ways:

  1. Open Fit33 → Settings and choose Apple Health
  2. Grant the categories you want to share in the iOS permission sheet

Workouts you record in Fit33 are written back to Apple Health, and cardio recorded elsewhere (Apple Watch, other apps) flows into Fit33 through Health.

5. Strava

Connect Strava in Settings → Strava (or via the Connect Strava card on the Cardio page). Once connected:

  • Your runs and rides appear in Fit33 shortly after you finish them
  • Strava activities count toward quests, league points, streaks, and challenges — same as native workouts
  • Native Fit33 runs are pushed to Strava automatically

See the Cardio guide for the full picture.

6. Fitbit, WHOOP & Oura

  • Fitbit (Settings → Fitbit): steps, heart rate, and sleep.
  • WHOOP (Settings → WHOOP): recovery score, HRV, strain, sleep stages, SpO2, skin temperature, and workouts. Recovery data powers the Dashboard recovery widget — when your recovery is deep in the red zone, Fit33 suggests light activity instead of heavy training.
  • Oura (Settings → Oura): sleep, readiness, HRV, and activity summaries.

Each connects via OAuth — you sign in with the provider's own credentials and approve the data categories. You can disconnect at any time from the same Settings screen or the provider's dashboard.

7. InBody & Bluetooth Equipment

  • InBody (Settings → InBody): import body-composition scans into your body-comp history.
  • Bluetooth equipment: compatible treadmills, bikes, and rowers can connect during indoor cardio to stream live speed, distance, cadence, watts, and heart rate.

8. No Double-Counting

Worried about a run showing up twice because it's in both Strava and Apple Health? Don't be — Fit33 dedupes workouts across sources, so the same session is only saved (and credited) once.

9. Exporting Your Data

Your data belongs to you. Go to Settings → Download Data to export everything tied to your account. Cloud backup runs automatically whenever you're signed in.

10. Common Questions

My steps show zero — what's wrong?

Almost always a HealthKit permission issue. Check iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Fit33 and make sure the read categories are enabled. See Troubleshooting for the full walkthrough.

How are PRs detected?

Automatically — every logged set is checked against your history. Fit33 tracks both weight PRs and volume PRs (weight × reps), and shows a 🏆 badge when you beat a previous best.

Do wearable workouts count toward my streak and quests?

Yes. Cardio from any source — Fit33 native, Apple Watch, Strava, or anything routed through Apple Health — counts toward streaks, daily quests, league points, and challenges.
